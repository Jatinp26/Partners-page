        Frequently asked questions.

        Common questions about the Canton Partners Program.

        Is there a fee to apply, and is Canton Foundation membership required?
        No. There is no fee to apply to the Canton Partners Program, and Canton Foundation membership is not currently required.
        Can an organization apply for more than one partner category?
        Yes. Organizations may apply for multiple categories and should indicate each relevant category in their application. For guidance on category selection, contact partners@canton.foundation.
        What does the term “Canton engineering team” mean?
        The Canton engineering team consists of the engineers working on an organization’s Canton projects, rather than the organization as a whole. The 15% certification requirement applies to this team.
        Who reviews applications?
        Applications are reviewed by the Canton Foundation’s partnerships and developer relations teams against the credentials required for each category. Approved partners are then listed in the Canton Partners Directory.
        Can organizations already listed on the Canton Ecosystem page apply?
        Yes. The Ecosystem page lists all organizations building on Canton, while the Canton Partners Directory lists only those that have earned Canton Partners credentials.
        What are the requirements for remaining listed?
        Partners must keep their profiles up to date, confirm annually that they continue to hold the credentials for their category, and participate in monthly check-ins with the program team.
        How do institutions connect with partners?
        Banks, asset managers, and market infrastructures use the Canton Partners Directory to identify partners on Canton and request introductions.
        Does the Canton Foundation recommend specific partners?
        No. The Canton Partners Directory presents verified credentials and delivery records, allowing institutions to select partners independently.
