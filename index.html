      Frequently asked questions.

      Eligibility, certification and listing for the Canton Partners Program.

      Do we need to be a Canton Foundation member, or pay to join?
      No. Applying to the Canton Partners Program is free, and Canton Foundation membership is not currently required.
      Which category should we apply for? Can we apply for more than one?
      Yes, you can apply for more than one category. When you express interest, tell us which categories fit your business. If you’re unsure, contact partners@canton.foundation.
      Who counts as our “Canton engineering team”?
      The engineers on your Canton projects, not the whole company. The 15% certification threshold is measured against this team.
      Who reviews our application?
      The Foundation’s partnerships and developer relations teams evaluate your application against the credentials for your selected category. Once approved, your profile is published in the Canton Partners Dashboard.
      We’re already on the Ecosystem page. Can we apply?
      Yes. The Ecosystem page lists everyone building on Canton; the Canton Partners Dashboard lists organizations that have met the Canton Partners credentials.
      How do we stay listed?
      Partners keep their profile up to date, confirm each year that they still meet the credentials for their category, and join monthly check-ins with the program team.
      How do institutions connect with partners?
      Banks, asset managers and market infrastructures use the Canton Partners Dashboard to find partners on Canton, and can request an introduction to the partner that fits their project.
      Does the Foundation recommend specific partners?
      No. The Canton Partners Dashboard shows verified credentials and delivery records, so institutions can choose for themselves.
