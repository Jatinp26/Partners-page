What It Takes & What You Get

Requirements depend on your partner type. Recognition is the same for every Canton Partner.

What It Takes

TECHNOLOGY & IMPLEMENTATION

  • A delivery or solution lead attends the business case workshops; every engineer on the track attends the technical labs.
  • At least 2 engineers or 15% of the Canton team, whichever is greater, hold a current Daml 3 certificate.
  • A validator node running, a DAR deployed and a smart contract executed on DevNet.
  • Evidence of delivery: at least one completed POC or live deployment on Canton.
  • Annual confirmation that the certification threshold is still met.

Frequently Asked Questions

Eligibility, certification and listing for the Canton Partners Program.

Do we need Daml experience to apply?
No. Teams new to Canton start on the Daml 3 Foundations path and learn from first principles.
We built on Daml 2. Which track do we take?
The Daml 2→3 Bridge, if you can show prior Daml 2 delivery work or Daml 2 certified engineers. If in doubt, start with Foundations.
Do older Daml certifications count?
Partner recognition is based on the current Daml 3 certifications. Earlier certifications cover concepts that have changed in Canton Network 3.x.
How much does certification cost?
For the tracks that require certification there is no cost. For those that would like to pursue it outside the program requirements, the cost is $150 per exam.
What about the rest of the tracks?
Criteria are coming soon. Register interest and we'll contact you when they open.
We're already on the Ecosystem page. Can we apply?
Yes. The Ecosystem page lists everyone building on Canton; the partner directory lists organizations that have met the Canton Partners criteria.
How long does certification take?
Typically two to four weeks for a committed team.
Does the Foundation recommend specific partners?
No. The directory shows verified credentials and delivery records, so institutions can choose for themselves.
How do we stay listed?
Partners confirm each year that they still meet the requirements.
